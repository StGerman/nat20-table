After a pick from a numbered list (a road card's choice, an option in or out of a fight, a reaction), the story kept only `> 1`; out of a fight the list then goes, so the scrollback no longer said what was chosen. Done means the echo is the list line the number named (`> 1. Навалиться плечами на колесо и толкать.`), y or n on a reaction echoes `> да` or `> нет`, and a number no line names, words and commands stay as typed. The number stays so a pick reads apart from the human's own words.
