SwiftUI shell design §4 PR 3, §2 (page, native mode), decision 0093. Today the page always opens its own socket and draws its own bar, and no shell can draw its controls. Opened with `?shell=native`, the page opens no socket and draws no bar; it takes host lines from the shell, runs the same reducer, and after each line posts the chrome state (`chromeOf(view)`: what the dock, the chips and the sheets show now, as plain data). A press from the shell ("option n", "typed text", "reaction yes or no", a command) comes in through the page's entry points; the page builds the intent and hands the message back for the socket. The page in a browser is unchanged. The spike's `game/src/page/chrome.ts` and its `main.ts` changes (table #480, branch `table/glass-table-hybrid-shell-spike`) are the start, reviewed.

Owns from design §5 ("What the spike skipped"):
- **Recorded wires hold four kinds of ask.** A wire that holds every `HumanPrompt` kind (reaction, the Narrator's question, the pause, surrender, level-up, a road step among them), and a check that what the page posts for each press passes the wire schema.
- **A reaction's "yes" takes the first answer.** A reaction that offers more than one answer shows each, in the page and in the chrome state.

Depends on: nothing open (the page, AGT-142, and the seat wire's schema, AGT-141, are on main).

Check (design §3 rows 1 and 2): every kind of ask is answered through the shell's entry points on Playwright's WebKit in the `page` job, and the posted intent passes the wire schema; the page in a browser replays as before: test, no model.
