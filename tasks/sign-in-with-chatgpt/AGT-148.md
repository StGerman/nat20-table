Decision 0105 and the 4a brief (StGerman/nat20-app `docs/brief.md`, §7 step 4): sign-in moves from the table server to the Swift client, which lands in StGerman/nat20-app, the iPhone first. It is the open-source SIWC flow as the iPhone spike ran it (nat20-app #4, `ios/SignInSpike/`): authorize at `auth.openai.com/api/accounts/authorize` with `client_id=dynamic_agent_client`, `agent_name_hint` and `ext_agent_host_id` on the first sign-in and the issued `oaiapp_` id after; PKCE S256; the listener on `127.0.0.1:1455` inside the app; the in-app sheet by default and Safari as the fallback; code exchange and rotating refresh at `/oauth/token` with `resource=https://api.openai.com/v1`; the tokens in the Keychain, this device only; sign-out by revocation. The spike review's three fixes go in: write with `SecItemUpdate` and check its status, read the listener up to the first CRLF, and make `Loopback` Swift 6 safe.
