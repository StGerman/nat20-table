`ResponsesSession` replaces `AgentSession` (`game/src/agents/sdk-session.ts`) for the Narrator, the players, the describer and the suggester: the seat tools as function tools from their zod shapes, our own tool loop over a streamed `POST /v1/responses` with `store: false` and the whole history each call (no server ids in it), `settleOn`, per-purpose timeouts, `cancel`, partial previews, usage records with cached tokens, the turn tracer, and errors mapped to the seat's stops. Run profiles name ChatGPT models (terra by default); preflight checks the ChatGPT sign-in instead of Claude Code; `AgentSession` stays for the narration judge only (decision 0091). A 401 refreshes the token (AGT-148) and retries once before it stops the seat. In the Narrator's `narrate` and `combat_over` wakes, the settling `narrate` ends our loop: its tool result is kept in the history for the next turn, and no further call is made (AGT-137: the Claude loop's closing call after it was 40 to 46% of the Narrator's calls in runs of 2026-11-24 and 11-25, and 119 of 120 calls after `narrate` in those wakes were text only). Other wakes keep calling until the model stops, since `table_turn` goes on with `remember` and `load_scene`. Done means scope §4 rows 2 and 3 pass on a recorded stream.
