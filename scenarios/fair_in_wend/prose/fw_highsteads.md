# The Highsteads

**Read aloud.** Evening at the Highsteads: wind off the hills, peat smoke, sheep complaining behind a stone wall. Odda Vell's wagon stands in the farmyard, heaped with sacks of the hill farms' wheat under an oiled cloth, two big horses steaming in the cold. Odda counts a day's pay into each of your hands, coin by coin, and nods at the track that drops away down the slope towards Wendford. "Fair starts the day after tomorrow. We go at first light. Wheat doesn't walk."

**What the characters can notice.**
- The track down is steep, rutted and eight miles long; the wagon will take most of the day on it.
- The shepherd at the fold gate keeps looking up at the ridges. With a good look at the hills (the `ridge_smoke` check), a character sees a thread of smoke on the far ridge where no farm is: someone is camping up there.
- Reading the sky (the `read_the_sky` check) says rain by evening, so the track will be worse tomorrow than today.

**How to run it.** A short scene on the evening of day 1: the contract is made, the advance is paid (the engine's lines show it), and the party meets Odda and whoever lives here. Odda means to sleep here and leave at first light: the hamlet has no inn, so it is a camp in the farmyard. The Blades call the night and the start themselves; if they want to go down the track in the dark, Odda says it is their necks and her horses, and goes along.
