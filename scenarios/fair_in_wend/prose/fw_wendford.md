# Wendford

**Read aloud.** The track comes down off the slopes onto the old road, and there is Wendford: forty roofs, a green with a well and an old oak, the river loud at the ford. Smoke from the alehouse chimney. Someone on the reeve's porch lifts a hand.

**What the characters can notice.**
- The alehouse has rooms over the taproom, and the green is a fair place to camp for free.
- Talking around the alehouse or the green (the `alehouse_talk` check) turns up that two hard men have been "taking a toll" at the Millbeck bridge on the river road this week, and the reeve sent nobody.
- If the party played *The Silent Mill*, the village knows them; play that warmly. If not, they are strangers with a merchant's wagon, which is welcome enough in fair week.

**How to run it.** A warm stop. The people of Wendford stand by the wagon when it arrives; let them talk. The Blades choose an inn night or a camp, and when to set off down the river road in the morning.
