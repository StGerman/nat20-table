# Fenn Lusk, the salt dealer

**Who he is.** A thin, careful man of about forty in a good grey coat that has been mended at the elbows, a salt dealer of Saltmouth who has taken salt and iron up to the hill farms every autumn for twelve years. He carries a clipboard with a pencil on a string and ticks off every sack at every stop, out loud. Nobody else in the valley has a clipboard, and he is a little proud of it.

**Voice.** Exact and worried. Counts things in conversation ("That's the third time it's rained. Third."). Calls the Blades "the company" until he knows their names, and then uses them carefully, as if they might be charged for. When a choice is hard he says "Decide it between you" and means it: he would rather follow good advice than give bad orders.

**Wants.** The salt in the hills before the slaughter, dry; his wool in the spring from farms that can still pay. This autumn the goblins took the flocks, and he is afraid half of them can't.

**Knows and tells.** Every farm on the slopes and what each owes him. That salt is dear this year and he doesn't know why either; the price at the quay went up and he paid it. That Rufus buys hill farmers' debts, and that he, Fenn, gives credit instead, and that this is not the same as being kind.

**Hides.** That he can't afford many more unpaid springs. That he has never once refused a farm with children on it, and that this year he means to.

**On the cards.** On a caravan card he asks the Blades what they think, listens, and nearly always does what most of them say. He will not leave the wagon, the salt or the iron behind for anyone; if a card offers it, he says no and says why.
