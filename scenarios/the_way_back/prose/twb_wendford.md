# Wendford

**Read aloud.** The river road comes up to the ford, and there is Wendford again: forty roofs, the green, the well, the old oak, the alehouse chimney smoking. It is quieter than on the way down. People look at the wagon, and then at what is on it.

**What the characters can notice.**
- The alehouse has rooms over the taproom, and the green is a fair place to camp for free.
- Asking around what salt costs this year (the `price_talk` check) turns up that it is dearer than anyone remembers, that nobody knows why, and that half the village blames Saltmouth and the other half blames the goblins. Nobody is right, and you never say who would be.
- The reeve, Osric Hale, is on his porch. He has heard what happened at the Millbeck bridge on the way down, one way or another.

**How to run it.** A stop with weight in it. The people of Wendford stand by the wagon when it arrives, and an arrival card may come first. The Blades choose an inn night or a camp, and when to set off up the slope track in the morning.
