# The Way Back

A living-world contract for four level 2 or 3 characters, about three game days and up to two hours of play: an escort back up the Wend valley, from the Saltmouth fair to the hill farms, with salt and iron for the winter. The setting is in the shared lore (`the_wend_valley.md`); the map, the road cards and the people of each place belong to the world (`worlds/wend/`), not to this pack. It is the second contract of Milestone 11: the road remembers what the party did on the first one.

## What the party is told

Fenn Lusk, a salt dealer from Saltmouth, takes salt and iron up to the hill farms every autumn, before the slaughter, so they can salt their mutton for the winter and mend their ploughs; the farms pay him in wool in the spring. His drover has stayed at the fair. On the fair's last evening Odda Vell, whose wheat the Grey Lantern company brought down, hands the company on to him: two gold pieces a day each, one day paid now, the rest when the wagon stands in the farmyard at the Highsteads. The road runs up the river road past the Millbeck bridge to Wendford, then up the slope track: a night here, a long day, a night at Wendford, and the Highsteads on the third day.

## The truth (DM only)

- Fenn is honest, anxious and exact. The contract is what he said, and he pays it in full at the Highsteads. A Blade who falls on the road is paid for the days served.
- This autumn the goblins took the flocks off the ridges (the Hungry hills). Fenn is afraid half the farms he sells to can't pay in spring, and he is right. He sells on credit where Rufus buys debts: the Blades see the other side of the same trouble.
- Salt is dear this year and nobody in the valley knows why. The cause is off the map, far from Wend; never name it, and never let anyone on screen explain it. What the table sees is the price.
- The road has the party's own past on it: the Millbeck bridge, Wendford, the farm under the ridge, Gwyn at the fold gate. The cards that read what the party did open each payoff with one plain sentence of what happened last time; say it.

## Running this pack

- The road is the engine's: the clock, the miles, the cards, the nights and the pay. You narrate it and never move it on yourself: the Blades call the next stretch and the night, and give their word on each card.
- Fenn Lusk is not a creature in the engine. He drives the wagon, keeps the purse and his clipboard, and talks; nothing in a fight can hurt him, and no narration says it did. On a caravan card he asks the Blades what they think and nearly always does what most of them say; when he won't, say why in his voice.
- Odda stays at Saltmouth. In the opening scene she hands the Blades on; what she says about them comes from what they did on her contract (get_state lists the deeds). If this pack is played alone, on a new party, she says only that they come recommended.
- The people of each place (the world's place NPCs) stand by the wagon when the party arrives. get_state lists them with their notes.
- No haggling and no trade in this pack, except what a card puts on the table. The pay is the reward. Name no sum yourself; the engine's lines show the coin.
- When the wagon reaches the Highsteads and the Blades are paid, the contract is done and the run ends: close it as a scene, with Fenn's last tick on the clipboard and the hills going dark.

## Tone

Late autumn, rain and salt. The way back is the same road with the season turned: the fair's noise behind, the hills ahead and colder, and the people the party met on the way down still there, a little worse off or a little better, because of what the party did.
