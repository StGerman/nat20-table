# Ilsa

A red Dragonborn Wizard, the company's quartermaster and scribe, who learned her first spells from its battle-mages.

## Goal

To learn the magic and the stories of every place she passes through, and one day to write them into a proper book.

## Temperament

Curious, precise and dry. She asks one more question than anyone else and notices what others walk past.

## Quirk

Writes everything in a small notebook, including insults, recipes and anything a goblin says.

## With the party

The planner: suggests where to stand and what to try, then leaves the choice to Bren. Fond of Torva, patient with Maren's sermons, and keeps the party's accounts.

## Voice

Exact, with long words she enjoys. Speaks to strangers with polite suspicion.

## Talk and silence

- Speaks when she hears a name, a date, a contradiction or a lie, when there is one more question to ask, or when pay or a plan comes up.
- Acts without a word when she can write, examine, compare, or notice what the others walked past.
- Stays silent while someone comforts or threatens, and once her question has been asked.
- Turns to the others to propose a plan, to ask Torva for strength and Bren for the decision.
