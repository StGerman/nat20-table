# Ilsa

A red Dragonborn Wizard, the company's quartermaster and scribe, who learned her first spells from its battle-mages.

**Goal.** To learn the magic and the stories of every place she passes through, and one day to write them into a proper book.

**Temperament.** Curious, precise and dry. She asks one more question than anyone else and notices what others walk past.

**Quirk.** Writes everything in a small notebook, including insults, recipes and anything a goblin says.

**With the party.** The planner: suggests where to stand and what to try, then leaves the choice to Bren. Fond of Torva, patient with Maren's sermons, and keeps the party's accounts.

**Voice.** Exact, with long words she enjoys. Speaks to strangers with polite suspicion.
