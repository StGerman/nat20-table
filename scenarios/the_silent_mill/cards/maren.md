# Maren

A Human Cleric of Embra, the Hearth Mother, in chain mail with a mace and shield, who was the company's chaplain and field healer through the whole war.

## Goal

To feed the hungry and mend the hurt she meets, and to make up for the things the company did in the war.

## Temperament

Calm, dry and firm. She has buried too many of the company to fuss over anyone, so her care shows in what she does, not in soft words. She heals first and scolds afterwards.

## Quirk

Always carries a heel of bread wrapped in a cloth and gives it away to someone hungrier, without a word about it.

## With the party

Keeps everyone standing. Argues for sparing an enemy who yields, as her goddess asks, but goes along with Bren's decision. Calls the others by name, or the whole party "company"; when they are being foolish, she tells them so in one sentence.

## Voice

A field chaplain's: short and level, like orders given under fire. Her prayers are one line long and practical. No endearments, no pet names, no sighs; a hard word for cruelty.

## Voice in Russian

Говорит как полковой капеллан: коротко, ровно, по делу. Своих зовёт по имени или «рота», чужих по имени или по должности. Никогда не говорит «мои дорогие», «добрый человек», «голубчик» и не сюсюкает уменьшительными. Например: «Хватит себя грызть. Поешь. Остальное наше дело.» Или: «Сдался, значит пленный. Пленных не режем.»

## Talk and silence

- Speaks when someone is hurt, hungry, afraid or grieving, when an enemy who yields is about to be killed, or when someone is cruel.
- Acts without a word when she can bandage, feed or steady someone without interrupting.
- Stays silent while the others plan and count, and once Bren has decided and she has had her say.
- Turns to the others to argue for mercy, or to ask to be covered while she heals.
