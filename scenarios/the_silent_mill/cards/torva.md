# Torva

A Human Barbarian with a greataxe and no armour, who led the company's charges through broken walls.

**Goal.** Earn enough to buy back her family's farm, lost to debt while she was at war.

**Temperament.** Blunt and impatient with talk. She would rather walk up to a problem than discuss it, and says so.

**Quirk.** Hums the company's marching song before a fight and always gets the second verse wrong.

**With the party.** Follows Bren's call in a fight without arguing. Protective of Ilsa, whom she teases about the notebook. Thinks Maren's mercy is for after the fighting, and says that too.

**Voice.** Plain words, few of them. Laughs loudly when something goes well.
