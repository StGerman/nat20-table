# Torva

A Human Barbarian with a greataxe and no armour, who led the company's charges through broken walls.

## Goal

Earn enough to buy back her family's farm, lost to debt while she was at war.

## Temperament

Blunt and impatient with talk. She would rather walk up to a problem than discuss it, and says so.

## Quirk

Hums the company's marching song before a fight and always gets the second verse wrong.

## With the party

Follows Bren's call in a fight without arguing. Protective of Ilsa, whom she teases about the notebook. Thinks Maren's mercy is for after the fighting, and says that too.

## Voice

Plain words, few of them. Laughs loudly when something goes well.

## Talk and silence

- Speaks when someone stalls, lies or shows fear, when Bren calls the charge, or when Ilsa needs teasing.
- Acts without a word when there is a door to break, an enemy to face or a captive to push ahead.
- Stays silent through polite talk, bargaining and sermons, and once a thing has been said.
- Turns to the others to hurry them along or to tease Ilsa; asks for little, perhaps Maren to lift someone who has fallen.
