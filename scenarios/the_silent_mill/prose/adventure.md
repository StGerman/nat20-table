# The Silent Mill

A one-shot for four level 2 characters, about 90 minutes of play: a social hook, a road with a trap and a chance at surprise, a fight in the mill yard, an interrogation, a fight in the cellar, and an epilogue. The setting is in the shared lore (`the_wend_valley.md`).

## What the party is told

The wheel of Pell's mill stopped turning two days ago. The miller's apprentice, Wenna Thorne, had gone up to run it alone and hasn't come back. The reeve of Wendford asks the party to find her.

## The truth (DM only)

- The Nettle Tooth goblins came down from the hills four days ago. The Hollow King wants tribute, and their boss, Gritch, has nothing to pay with. A mill full of grain will do.
- They took the mill the morning Wenna arrived. Before they caught her she dropped the sluice gate, so the wheel would stop and the village would notice. The silence is her message.
- Gritch keeps her tied to the millstone in the cellar, grinding grain by hand into the band's sacks, and as a hostage if the village comes. Four goblins and three wolves hold the yard. Gritch, three more goblins and two wolves are in the cellar. Giant rats nest in the grain chute.
- The reeve, Osric Hale, lent the miller money from his own purse for a new millstone this spring. He wants Wenna back, and he wants his loan safe; he isn't proud of the second part.

## Running this pack

- The scene files hold every check, DC, hazard, fight and spawn. When a player tries something one of the scene's check hints covers, ask for that check; otherwise use the SRD DC ladder through the engine. You never decide a success yourself.
- Wenna is not a creature in the engine. Nothing in the cellar fight can hurt her, and no narration says it did. She is free when the cellar fight ends with the party standing.
- No goblin flees. A goblin may surrender in the yard when the yard's surrender condition holds. In the cellar the band fights to the end, and Gritch never yields.
- The reward is in the fiction: the reeve pays, the village feeds the party. Name no sum and change no sheet; loot is not part of this adventure.
- Keep each scene near its target time. If a talk runs long, let an NPC close it.
- If the party fights the band to the last in both fights, the adventure still works: Scene 4 becomes a search of the storeroom, and the boss is not warned.

## Tone

Grounded and a little warm. The village is frightened but decent; the goblins are dangerous, greedy and now and then funny. Describe the mill as a working place gone quiet: dust, flour, the smell of wet wood, the river louder than it should be without the wheel.
