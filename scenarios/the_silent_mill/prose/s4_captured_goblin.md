# Scene 4: the captured goblin

**Read aloud.** Inside the mill door is a long storeroom, dim and floury. Sacks are slit open and grain is trodden into the boards. Flour barrels stand by a cold hearth. Near it a hatch in the floor stands open over a ladder, and at the far end a stair goes down into the dark. From below comes a slow grinding, stone on stone, and a girl's voice saying something sharp.

**What the characters can notice**

- The grinding is a hand quern, not the great stones: someone below is being made to work.
- A post by the hearth, with rope, is a good place to tie a prisoner.
- Goblin bedding of sacking and straw: more goblins have slept here than were in the yard.

**Running it**

- If the party has a prisoner, run the interrogation with Snitt (`prose/npcs/snitt.md`). Threatening him is `interrogate_intimidation`; bargaining or promising mercy is `interrogate_persuasion`. Ask for one check, by the character doing the talking.
- On a success the engine sets `knows_cellar_layout`: Snitt gives up the boss's name, Gritch, that the girl is alive and tied at the millstone, how many are below, and that there is a back door to the millrace and rats in the grain chute.
- On a failure the engine sets `boss_warned`: Snitt shrieks a warning down the hatch before anyone can stop him, and the grinding stops. Gritch calls in a fourth goblin from the millrace, and it waits at the back door in Scene 5.
- Without a prisoner, the party searches the storeroom and finds the bedding, the hatch and the stair; nobody is warned.
- Offer a short rest here. The grinding below goes on; Gritch doesn't come up.
- The scene ends when the party goes down the stair. Snitt stays tied to the post.
