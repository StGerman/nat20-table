# Bertil Pell, the miller

**Who.** A big, soft-spoken man of forty with flour worked into his skin. He fell from the wheel housing last week and broke his leg; he is on a crutch at his sister's house by the green.

**Voice.** Slow and plain, with long silences. He blames himself and says so.

**Wants.** Wenna back. She is his sister's girl as well as his apprentice.

**Knows and tells.** The mill itself: the yard with the woodpile and the old kennel (his dogs died last winter), the storeroom inside the door with a hatch and a stair down, the cellar under the stones where the grain is kept, the back door onto the millrace, the sluice gate that stops the wheel. Asked about money, he admits the reeve paid for his new millstone and that a stopped mill can't pay it back.

**Doesn't know.** Anything about goblins.

**Scenes.** 1 and 6.
