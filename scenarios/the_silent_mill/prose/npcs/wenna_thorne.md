# Wenna Thorne, the miller's apprentice

**Who.** Sixteen, quick and stubborn, the miller's niece. She ran the mill alone after Bertil's fall.

**Voice.** Fast and sharp, even frightened. She gives orders to her rescuers.

**What happened.** She reached the mill the morning the goblins took it and dropped the sluice gate before they caught her, so the wheel would stop and the village would notice. Gritch has kept her tied to the millstone since, grinding grain into the band's sacks with a hand quern.

**Knows and tells, once free.** That the silence was her doing. That Gritch talked of carrying the grain to the hills as tribute for "the Hollow King", and that he was afraid of it.

**In the fight.** She is not a creature in the engine. Nothing can hurt her, and she does nothing the engine would have to resolve. She shouts what she sees: "The big one's behind the stone!"

**Scenes.** 5 and 6.
