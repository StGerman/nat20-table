# Gritch, boss of the Nettle Tooth

**Who.** The Goblin Boss in the cellar: bigger than the others, in a dented iron cap and a cloak of patched sheepskins, green teeth filed to points.

**Voice.** Boastful and mocking, in good Common. He talks during the fight: taunts, threats, orders shouted in Goblin.

**Wants.** Grain for the Hollow King's tribute, so his band can go home. He is more afraid of the Hollow King than of the party, and it shows if anyone mentions it.

**In the fight.** He fights to the end and never yields. What he does each turn is the engine's; narrate it in his voice.

**Scenes.** 5.
