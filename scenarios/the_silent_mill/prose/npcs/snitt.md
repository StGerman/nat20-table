# Snitt, the goblin who surrendered

**Who.** Whichever Nettle Tooth goblin threw down its blade in the yard. Skinny, green-toothed, with more Common than the others because he did the band's haggling.

**Voice.** Wheedling and fast, switching to Goblin when frightened. Calls the party "big masters". Bargains for everything, including his own life.

**Wants.** To live and to be let go. He hates Gritch, who takes his share.

**Knows.** The boss is Gritch, in the cellar with the girl at the millstone. She's alive. Below are three more goblins and two wolves, and rats in the grain chute that the band feeds scraps to. There's a back door onto the millrace. The band came for grain to pay the Hollow King's tribute.

**How the check plays.** He tells all of that on a success, haggling all the way. On a failure he shrieks a warning down the storeroom hatch (Scene 4 prose).

**Afterwards.** He stays tied to the post. What the party does with him is up to them; if they promised to let him go, the village will hold them to it, or not.

**Scenes.** 3 (as a goblin in the yard) and 4.
