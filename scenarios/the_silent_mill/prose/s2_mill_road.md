# Scene 2: the mill road

**Read aloud.** The lane runs between high hedges heavy with blackberries, the river somewhere to your left behind the alders. Where it forks at a mossy boundary stone, one way climbs on toward the mill and the other drops to the ford. It is very still.

**What the characters can notice**

- The mud of the lane is churned: something has come and gone this way more than once.
- Past the bend the mill's roof shows over the hedges. No smoke, and no sound of the wheel.

**Running it**

- Following the tracks or watching the path (`find_tracks`): on a success, the tracker reads small clawed feet, many of them, and paw prints as big as a hand, and spots a snare of sharpened stakes under the leaves at the hedge bend in time to lead the party round it. On a failure the engine springs the snare (`road_snare`) on that character; narrate the stakes and the save as the engine rolled it.
- Scouting ahead (`spot_lookouts`): on a success, the scout sees a goblin crouched on the woodpile and another in the mill doorway, both watching the lane, and the party can come at the yard unseen. The engine sets `party_ambushes`, and the goblins start Scene 3's fight surprised. On a failure nothing changes, and the party walks up the lane in plain sight.
- Ask for each check once; the party chooses who tries. If nobody tries, they simply arrive.
- The scene ends at the mill gate.
