# Scene 5: the mill cellar

**Read aloud.** The stairs come down into a low stone cellar lit by a single lantern. Grain bins line the walls, and at the far end the great millstone stands on its pit. A girl in a flour-white apron is tied to it, a hand quern at her knees. Beside her, a goblin in a dented iron cap and a cloak of patched sheepskins grins with green teeth. "Ah," he says. "The village sends heroes. Good. Heroes carry money."

**What the characters can notice**

- The boss is Gritch. Two wolves crouch in front of the millstone, two goblins stand either side of it, and another waits by the back door to the millrace.
- Wenna is tied, frightened and furious. She shouts what she can see to the party.
- A wooden grain chute comes down from the floor above; something in it is scratching.

**Running it**

- Start `cellar_fight` when the party reaches the foot of the stairs, or at once if anyone attacks from them.
- A warned Gritch has a fourth goblin at the back door (`extra_warrior`); the engine places it when the fight starts.
- Giant rats pour out of the grain chute on round 3 (`chute_rats`) unless a party member is already Bloodied or down; the engine decides and places them.
- Gritch taunts, gives orders in Goblin, and fights to the end. So do his goblins. Nothing here surrenders or flees.
- Nothing in the fight can hurt Wenna. When it ends with the party standing, they can untie her.
- The scene ends when the party leaves with Wenna. She tells them on the way that she dropped the sluice gate herself, and that she heard Gritch talk of tribute for "the Hollow King".
