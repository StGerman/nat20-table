# Scene 6: back on the green

**Read aloud.** By the time you reach the green, half of Wendford is waiting. Somebody has raised the sluice: behind you, faint at first and then steady, the mill wheel is turning again. Bertil Pell drops his crutch to hold his apprentice, and the reeve comes down off his porch with the common chest's purse in both hands.

**What the characters can notice**

- The village is grateful and loud. Bread and cider appear from nowhere.
- The reeve is relieved twice over, and a sharp character can see it.

**Running it**

- Let each character have a moment: a villager's thanks, a question about the fight, Wenna's own thanks.
- The reeve pays as he promised. Name no sum; the engine's purses don't change in this adventure.
- The hook: Wenna repeats what Gritch said about the Hollow King, and an old shepherd says the barrows in the Hollin Hills have been lit at night all autumn. Leave it there.
- End the adventure with a closing line of narration to the table. There is no exit.
