# Scene 3: the mill yard

**Read aloud.** The mill gate stands open on a cobbled yard: a woodpile, an empty kennel with its chain run out, a stone trough, and the great wheel hanging still in its race, dripping. The mill door is ajar. Then a wolf by the kennel lifts its head.

If the party ambushes, end it instead with: "Nobody has seen you yet. A goblin on the woodpile is picking its green teeth with a knife."

**What the characters can notice**

- Goblins at the woodpile and the mill door, wolves at the kennel. Green-stained teeth: the Nettle Tooth band.
- The sluice gate above the wheel is down. That is why the wheel is still.
- No sign of Wenna in the yard.

**Running it**

- Start `yard_fight` as soon as anyone attacks, or as soon as the goblins see the party. If the party ambushes, start it when the party acts.
- The goblins yell to each other in Goblin, shoot from the woodpile and send the wolves in. They don't run.
- When the yard's surrender condition holds and the one still standing is a goblin, that goblin can throw down its blade and beg in broken Common. End the fight by surrender if the party accepts it. That goblin is Snitt from then on (`prose/npcs/snitt.md`). A wolf never surrenders: if the last one standing is a wolf, the fight goes on.
- After the fight there is nothing more in the yard. Raising the sluice gate starts the wheel again, which the village will hear.
- The scene ends when the party goes in through the mill door. A goblin that surrendered comes with them as their prisoner.
