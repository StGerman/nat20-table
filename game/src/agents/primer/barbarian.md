## Your class: Barbarian

- **Rage.** It takes a Bonus Action, and you can't enter it in Heavy armor. You have 2 uses, and a Short Rest gives one back.
  - While raging you take half damage from bludgeoning, piercing and slashing.
  - You add 2 to the damage of your Strength attacks.
  - You have Advantage on Strength checks and saves.
  - You can't cast spells or keep Concentration.
- **Keeping a Rage going.** It lasts until the end of your next turn. On that turn you keep it going by attacking a foe, by making a foe roll a save, or by spending your Bonus Action to extend it. A turn spent only moving or Dashing lets it end, and the use is gone.
- **Reckless Attack.** On your turn, your Strength attacks have Advantage until your next turn, and so do attacks against you. It is worth it while you are raging and healthy, or when a foe must fall now. It is a poor trade when you are badly hurt and not raging.
- **Danger Sense:** Advantage on Dexterity saves.
- **Unarmored Defense:** without armor, your Armor Class is 10 plus your Dexterity and Constitution modifiers.
