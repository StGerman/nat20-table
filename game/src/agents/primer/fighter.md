## Your class: Fighter

- **Second Wind.** It takes a Bonus Action and heals you 1d10 plus your Fighter level. You have 2 uses, and a Short Rest gives one back. Use it while you are hurt; it does nothing once you have fallen.
- **Tactical Mind.** When you fail an ability check, you can use your reaction and a use of Second Wind to add 1d10 to it. If the check still fails, the use isn't spent.
- **Action Surge.** It gives you one extra action on your turn, for example two attacks, or an attack and a Help. You have 1 use, and a Short Rest gives it back.
- **Fighting Style.** Defense adds 1 to your Armor Class while you wear armor.
- **Front line.** You are built to stand between the foes and your weaker companions. Where you stand decides who the foes can reach.
