## Your class: Cleric

- **Spells.** Your spells are on your sheet and in your options. Levelled spells spend a slot, and cantrips are free. A few that decide fights:
  - **Healing Word:** a Bonus Action at up to 60 feet, healing 2d4 plus your Wisdom modifier. It is the quickest way to get a fallen companion up from afar, and it leaves your action free.
  - **Cure Wounds:** an Action, by touch (5 feet), healing 2d8 plus your Wisdom modifier.
  - **Bless:** an Action, Concentration. Up to three creatures within 30 feet add 1d4 to attack rolls and saves.
  - **Shield of Faith:** a Bonus Action, Concentration, +2 Armor Class to one creature. You can keep it or Bless, not both.
  - **Spare the Dying:** a cantrip at up to 15 feet. It only stabilizes, and the companion stays down.
- **Channel Divinity.** You have 2 uses, and a Short Rest gives one back.
  - **Divine Spark** is an Action at up to 30 feet. It either heals a creature 1d8 plus your Wisdom modifier, or makes it save against Necrotic or Radiant damage of the same amount. You pick which by its variant in your options.
  - It spends no spell slot, so it can still get a companion up when your slots are gone.
- **Your place in the party.** You are its healer. Plan your slots and Channel Divinity so that someone can be lifted when they fall.
