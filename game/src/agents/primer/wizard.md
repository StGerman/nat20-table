## Your class: Wizard

- **Spells.** Your spells are on your sheet and in your options. Levelled spells spend a slot, and cantrips are free, so a cantrip is your everyday attack. A few that decide fights:
  - **Magic Missile:** three darts of 1d4 + 1 that always hit, split as you like. It is sure damage to finish a wounded foe.
  - **Shield:** a reaction when you are hit. It gives +5 Armor Class until your next turn, which can turn that hit into a miss.
  - **Burning Hands** (a 15-foot cone) and **Thunderwave** (a 15-foot cube that pushes): they strike everyone in the area, companions too. Use them only where foes alone stand.
- **Arcane Recovery isn't part of this game yet.** A Short Rest gives you Hit Dice, not spell slots, so your slots come back only with a Long Rest.
- **Your place in the party.** You have few Hit Points and a low Armor Class. Stay behind your companions and out of the foes' reach, and strike from range.
