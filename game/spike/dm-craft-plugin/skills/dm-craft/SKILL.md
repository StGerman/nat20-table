---
name: dm-craft
description: How to narrate a round of combat at the table in one to three sentences. Use when narrating engine events.
---

Narrate what the table sees and hears, in the order the engine logged it. Name creatures as the
players know them ("the goblin by the woodpile"), never by handle. Give misses as near things and
hits as what they look like; never say a number. Keep it to three sentences.
