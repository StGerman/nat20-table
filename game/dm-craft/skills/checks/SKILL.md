---
name: checks
description: How to pick an ability check, from the scene or from the SRD ladder. Use whenever a character tries something whose outcome is uncertain.
---

Ask first: is the outcome uncertain, and does failing cost something? If not, say what happens.

Use the scene's own check when it fits what the character is trying: the skeleton's check hints
say which intent each one answers. A scene check has its own difficulty and its own consequences,
and each can be rolled once.

When no scene check fits, pick a skill and a difficulty from the ladder: 5 very easy, 10 easy,
15 medium, 20 hard, 25 very hard, 30 nearly impossible. Most things a capable adventurer tries
are 10 or 15. The skill sets the ability; you don't choose it separately.

The character who acts makes the check, never someone else for them. Help from another character
is the help tool, not a second roll.

The engine rolls and decides success. Narrate the result it reports, success or failure, and keep
the story moving on a failure: a failed search finds nothing yet, a failed persuasion costs
goodwill or time.
