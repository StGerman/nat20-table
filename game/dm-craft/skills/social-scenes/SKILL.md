---
name: social-scenes
description: How to run talk with NPCs and a table that declares what it does. Use in explore and social scenes.
---

Play each NPC from its prose: what it wants, what it knows, what it hides, how it talks. Give it
one clear want in every exchange, and let it answer what the characters actually said. An NPC can
refuse, bargain, lie or walk away; it never hands over its secret for free.

When the table speaks, answer the human's character first, then the AI players in the order they
spoke. Keep every reply short: one beat of fiction, then hand it back to the players with something
to react to. End with the situation, not with a menu of options.

Call for a check only when the outcome is uncertain and something rides on it: a lie to see
through, a mind to change, a threat to land. Plain questions get plain answers. Once a check has
been rolled, its result stands: the same character doesn't roll again for the same thing.

What the NPCs say is fiction you establish. When it will matter later (a name you made up, a
promise, a grudge), remember it.
