# 0018. Run an original one-shot, The Silent Mill, not a published adventure

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-16
- **Decided by:** Claude recommended in the Demo Run scope; Stas accepted the scope 2026-10-16

## Context

Stas asked for a newbie one-shot from DMs Guild for a 1-2 hour run. DMs Guild adventures are licensed for personal use; WotC's free PDFs are WotC text. The demo must be shown and possibly shipped ([0011](0011-content-licensing.md)).

## Options

### A. A DMs Guild adventure (best fit: A Most Potent Brew)
- Pro: written and tested.
- Con: cannot appear in a demo we show, record or ship; its text cannot go into the repo or prompts; 2014 rules and a custom boss.

### B. An original one-shot fitted to Nat20's coverage
- Pro: we own it (CC-BY-4.0); every monster is SRD; scenes fit the engine and the time budget.
- Con: we write and balance it.

## Decision

B: *The Silent Mill*. 6 scenes (social hook, exploration, two goblin-and-wolf fights, an interrogation, wrap-up), about 90 minutes, SRD monsters only (Goblin Warrior, Goblin Boss, Wolf, Giant Rat; Bugbear ruled out because it needs grappling). A Most Potent Brew stays for Stas's private tests.

## Consequences

- Scenario, pregens, maps and lore live in our repo under CC-BY-4.0.
- Encounter budget: Fight 1 350 XP (Low), Fight 2 450 XP (between Low and Moderate).

## Revisit when

The demo is accepted and a longer adventure is needed.
