# 0009. 2024 rules via SRD 5.2.1; 2014 data only fills missing fields

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-16
- **Decided by:** Stas ("Is it 5.5e based rules?", then "Okay, sounds great!" in the mechanics backlog thread)

## Context

Two rulesets have open-licensed SRDs: 2014 (SRD 5.1) and 2024 (SRD 5.2.1, both CC-BY-4.0). Nat20 targets 2024. Structured data for 2024 is thinner: 5e-bits' 2024 spells have no save, area or healing fields.

## Options

### A. 2024 rules (SRD 5.2.1)
- Pro: current rules; matches Nat20; weapon mastery, exhaustion as -2 per level, surprise as initiative disadvantage.
- Con: thinner structured data; more hand-encoding.

### B. 2014 rules (SRD 5.1)
- Pro: richer structured data and tools.
- Con: outdated rules; conflicts with Nat20.

### C. Mix per subsystem
- Con: inconsistent rules no player expects.

## Decision

A. 2014 data only pre-fills a field the 2024 data lacks, and each pre-filled entry is checked against the 2024 text before it goes in.

## Consequences

- CLAUDE.md Rule 7. Known data bugs are listed there (Goblin Warrior's extra 1d4 is Advantage-only; three monsters with scrambled SRD scores).
- Data sources are chosen for 2024 coverage ([0010](0010-data-sources.md)).

## Revisit when

A later SRD revision is published under an open licence.
