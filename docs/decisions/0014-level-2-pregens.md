# 0014. Four level 2 pregens: Fighter, Cleric, Wizard, optional Barbarian

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-16
- **Decided by:** Claude proposed in the Demo Run scope; Stas accepted the scope 2026-10-16

## Context

The mechanics backlog suggested level 3. Nat20 implements no level 3 subclass (no Berserker, Evoker or Cleric domain), and subclasses are out of scope. A 90-minute run with no healer tends to end in a wipe.

## Options

### A. Level 1
- Pro: simplest characters.
- Con: swingy; one goblin crit downs a character.

### B. Level 2
- Pro: Action Surge and Reckless Attack, which Nat20 has; less swingy than level 1; no subclass needed.
- Con: still needs Cleric levels 1-2 built.

### C. Level 3
- Con: needs subclasses Nat20 does not have.

## Decision

B, with pregens only (no character creation): Human Fighter (longsword and shield) for the human, Human Cleric (mace and shield), Dragonborn Wizard, and optionally a Human Barbarian. With only two AI players, drop the Barbarian, never the Cleric.

## Consequences

- Build list: Cleric 1-2 and 6 spells, Human species, Shield, Mace, Chain Shirt, Potion of Healing (CHR, ITM rows).
- Encounters are budgeted for four level 2 characters.

## Revisit when

Nat20 gains level 3 subclasses and the scope grows beyond the demo.
