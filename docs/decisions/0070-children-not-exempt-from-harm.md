# 0070. Children are not exempt from harm on screen

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-12
- **Decided by:** Stas, thread "Bring companions and animals into fights" (2026-11-12 19:30Z and 19:32Z)

## Context

The bible's guardrail 4 said "Torture and harm to a child are never shown", and the `boy_on_tailboard` card note in `worlds/wend/deck/hills.json` told the DM "Never harm him on screen". While deciding which non-party beings exist in a fight (Lev Neiman's consult: the dog, the Merchant Odda Vell, the horses), Lev proposed a matching rule that a child never gets Hit Points in a fight. Stas answered the opposite: he would keep children's death possible, as in Fallout 1 and 2, and remove the rule.

## Options

### A. Keep the guardrail
- Pro: no risk of a scene a first-time player finds gratuitous; nothing to change.
- Con: a child on the road is safe by fiat, so "a consequence on the young" (Examples, Consequence 3) is never a real stake; the story protects someone the world would not.

### B. A child can die in the world, but never on screen
- Pro: the stake is real; the death arrives as news, and the table never watches it.
- Con: a death the party could have prevented happens off stage, which reads as the DM hiding the consequence.

### C. Drop the child half of the guardrail; torture stays banned
- Pro: the road treats a child like anyone else; a consequence on the young is a real one, as in Fallout 1 and 2.
- Con: a DM can now narrate a child hurt or killed; the voice guide has to keep it plain and short.

## Decision

C. Stas removed the rule. Torture stays never shown: he did not touch that half. A child's harm is told in one plain sentence, like any other death on the road, and never lingered on (voice guide).

## Consequences

- Guardrail 4 in `.claude/skills/zoya/references/commandments.md` and its row in `examples.md`, the line in `.claude/agents/zoya.md` and the line in `game/dm-craft/skills/voice/SKILL.md` change; "Never harm him on screen" leaves the `boy_on_tailboard` note.
- No rule that a child never gets Hit Points. Whether a child ever becomes a creature in a fight is a content choice per card, not a ban.
- The narration judge and the Vera test still apply to any scene where it happens.

## Revisit when

A Vera reading or a friends-and-family player stops at a child's death on screen, or a run shows a DM lingering on it.
